ROOF TILING · META LEAD ADS

133 roof tiling leads, straight from the account.

Two Meta lead campaigns for Western Roof Tiling produced 133 form leads on $3,350.79 of spend, a blended $25.19 per lead. The best campaign brought in 102 leads at $21.44 each.
