Google Ads for Plumbers: Campaign Structure and Lead Quality

Plumbing searches range from burst pipes to bathroom upgrades. Structure your account so each gets the right message, budget and response.

In short

Structure Google Ads for plumbers by intent — emergency, water heater, drain, repipe — rather than putting everything in one campaign. Use negative keywords, service-area and schedule controls, tracked calls and a qualifying landing page so spend goes to jobs you actually want.

Why structure matters

Plumbing searches vary enormously in value and urgency. Someone searching for a burst pipe will pay for fast help; someone searching for how to fix a dripping tap may never hire anyone. A single catch-all campaign mixes these together and tends to spend heavily on the wrong clicks.

Group campaigns by intent

  • Emergency: burst pipes, leaks, blocked drains and no hot water, with immediate calls to action.
  • Water heater: replacement and installation, usually higher-value.
  • Drains and sewer: cleaning, clearing and line repair.
  • Planned work: repiping, bathroom and kitchen plumbing.

Each group has its own budget, ad copy and landing page, so you can see what pays and move money accordingly.

Use negative keywords

Exclude searches that signal do-it-yourself, jobs, training or free advice, along with services you do not offer. Review search term reports regularly, because new irrelevant searches appear constantly.

Control location and schedule

Limit ads to the areas your technicians can reach and, if you cannot answer after hours, adjust the schedule or use an automated reply so enquiries are not wasted.

Track calls and forms

Many plumbing enquiries are phone calls. Call tracking and form tracking let you connect enquiries to campaigns and keywords, rather than relying on guesswork. See how to measure contractor lead generation ROI.

Improve lead quality after the click

A landing page that matches the search, a short qualifying form and a quick reply all lift quality. Fast replies matter most for emergencies, so consider AI appointment setting for out-of-hours enquiries.
