How Electrical Contractors Can Generate More Panel Upgrade Leads

Panel upgrades are planned, high-value jobs. Winning them is about the right trigger, the right questions and a quick reply.

In short

Panel upgrade leads come from homeowners who have an older panel or are adding load — an EV charger, heat pump, solar or an addition. Reach them with Google Ads when they search and Meta Ads before they do, ask qualifying questions about the home and timing, and reply fast so you win the estimate.

Who needs a panel upgrade?

The typical triggers are an older or crowded panel, repeated tripped breakers, a planned EV charger, a heat pump, solar or battery installation, or an addition that adds electrical load. Many homeowners do not realise their panel is the limiting factor until an installer or electrician tells them. That is both the opportunity and the challenge: part of your job is education.

Reach them in two ways

  • Google Ads for homeowners already searching for a panel upgrade or a related service.
  • Meta Ads for homeowners planning something that will need more capacity, using clear, educational creative about the signs a panel may need attention.

See the comparison in Facebook Ads vs Google Ads for electrical contractors.

Lead with a useful offer

An offer such as a panel and load assessment lowers the barrier for a homeowner who is not sure they need the upgrade. It also gives you a reason to visit, assess the work properly and prepare a real estimate. Avoid vague promises of savings or fixed prices that depend on the property.

Qualify before you drive out

A short form can ask about the type and age of the home, the symptoms or planned additions, location and timing. That lets you decide whether to book an estimate straight away, ask a follow-up question, or politely decline work outside your area.

Respond quickly and keep following up

Panel upgrades usually involve several quotes. A fast reply with appointment options and a reminder before the visit helps you stand out, and a follow-up after the estimate keeps the quote from going cold. This is where AI appointment setting helps.

Measure what matters

Track enquiries by job type through to booked estimates and signed jobs so you know whether panel upgrade campaigns are paying. For the wider approach, see our electrical contractor marketing page. Everything above is general guidance, not a promise of results.
