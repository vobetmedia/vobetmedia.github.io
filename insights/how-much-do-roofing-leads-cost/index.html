How Much Do Roofing Leads Cost? A Practical Guide

Cost per lead is the wrong number to anchor on. Here is how to compare roofing lead sources by what they actually deliver.

In short

There is no single price for a roofing lead: it varies by market, season, lead source and quality. A more useful figure is cost per booked inspection and per closed job. In one published Vobet Media case study, Meta campaigns produced leads from $19.28 and booked inspections at roughly $33 for one US client — a single example, not a benchmark.

Why there is no single answer

Search for the average cost of a roofing lead and you will find wildly different figures. That is because a lead can mean very different things: a shared enquiry sold to several roofers, an exclusive form fill from your own ads, or a phone call from a homeowner in your service area. Price depends on your market, the season, competition and how tightly the lead is qualified. Treat any number without that context with caution.

Three common lead sources

  • Shared marketplace leads. Sold per enquiry, often to several roofers at once. Price per lead can look low, but your chances depend on who calls first and on price.
  • Exclusive leads. Sold to you alone, usually at a higher price per lead, with better odds per enquiry.
  • Leads from your own campaigns. Cost per lead varies, but you own the account, the data and the follow-up, and you can optimise towards booked inspections.

Compare cost per job, not cost per lead

Divide the lead price by the share that become booked inspections, and then by the share of inspections that close. Illustrative example, not a client result: if a lead costs $50, 25% book an inspection and 30% of those close, that is 50 ÷ 0.25 ÷ 0.30 ≈ $667 per job. A cheaper lead that books and closes less often can cost far more per job than a pricier, better-qualified one.

One verified example

In our published roofing case study, Meta campaigns for one US business produced 74 leads and 33 booked inspections over two months. The lowest cost per lead in the account was $19.28, and appointments in one campaign came at about $33 each. That is one client, one market and one period — useful to show the method, not a benchmark for your area.

What moves cost per lead

  • The offer and creative in the ad
  • Whether a custom form or an instant form is used
  • Season, weather events and local competition
  • Service area size and targeting
  • How quickly each enquiry is answered
