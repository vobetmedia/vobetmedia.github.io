About Vobet Media

What is Vobet Media?

Vobet Media is a growth partner for D2C and B2B brands. It runs Meta and Google ads and email and SMS for D2C and ecommerce brands, lead generation and outbound for B2B companies, and AI automation for both.

Pricing and costs

How much does Vobet Media cost?

A monthly management fee plus your ad budget, which stays in your own ad account. Both are set together on the first call, before you commit to anything.

What is a good cost per lead?

It depends on your business and offer. Our published client accounts range from £3.68 to $29.24 per lead. Judge campaigns on cost per customer, not cost per lead alone. Read more →

How do I calculate cost per appointment?

Divide your cost per lead by the share of leads that book. For example, $30 per lead with one in three booking is about $90 per appointment. Read more →

How much does pay-per-appointment cost for home renovation companies?

There is no single market price; it depends on project type, area and how the vendor defines an appointment. Work out your own number from cost per lead and booking rate, and check exclusivity and cancellation terms. Read more →

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platform from your own account; our fee is separate.
