THE PROBLEM

"I've Tried Roofing Leads Before.
They Were Junk."

It is the objection every roofing contractor raises, and it is usually fair. The problem is almost never the lead source. It is what happens in the ninety minutes after the form is filled in — and in the two weeks after the estimate is handed over.

01

Leads that go cold on the roof

The crew is on a job all day. The homeowner fills in three forms, and whoever calls first wins the inspection. Nobody is calling first.

02

Estimates nobody chases

The inspection happens, the number is quoted, and the file goes quiet. No sequence, no reminder, no second touch. The job goes to the company that followed up.

03

Shared, resold, tyre-kicker leads

Lead vendors sell the same homeowner to four roofers. You pay for a race you did not enter and blame the channel when it does not convert.

04

Feast-or-famine months

Storm season is chaos, the off-season is silence, and there is no owned system generating steady local demand in between.
