1. Services & Deliverables

For clients on our standard contractor engagement, Vobet Media provides:

  • Strategy and offer review, including your service area, pricing, and past campaign data
  • Meta and/or Google ad campaigns built, written, and designed for your business — creative, copy, targeting, and landing pages
  • A custom qualifying form in place of generic instant-form lead capture, built to filter for genuine, in-area enquiries
  • Speed-to-lead follow-up — automated first contact on new enquiries, with a structured follow-up sequence for anyone who doesn't answer
  • Estimate-chasing follow-up on quotes sent, so leads aren't lost to a lack of a second touch
  • Weekly creative testing and budget optimisation across live campaigns
  • A weekly reporting call or update covering spend, leads, and booked appointments
  • Every ad reviewed and approved by you before it goes live

The exact mix of channels and deliverables is confirmed with you on the strategy call and during onboarding, based on your market, budget, and goals.

2. The 90-Day Performance Guarantee

Where quoted to a client, our guarantee is: 120 qualified estimates within 90 days of campaign launch, or we continue working at no additional management fee until that threshold is reached.

A "qualified estimate" means an appointment booked with a homeowner or business who:

  • Falls inside the service area and project types agreed during onboarding, and
  • Has confirmed a genuine interest in receiving a quote for the work.

The guarantee applies for as long as, during the 90-day period, the Client:

  • Maintains the ad spend agreed at onboarding, without extended pauses or reductions
  • Reviews and approves submitted ad creative within 48 hours of it being sent
  • Provides the photos, videos, or brand assets requested during onboarding within a reasonable time
  • Keeps the ad accounts and any tracking or forms we set up connected and unmodified

The guarantee covers booked, qualified estimates — not jobs closed, revenue generated, or a fixed number of impressions or clicks. What a homeowner decides after the estimate is outside our control and outside the scope of this guarantee. The 90-day window begins at campaign launch, not at the date of signing or onboarding.

To deliver the services and guarantee above, we need you to:

  • Provide access to the relevant ad accounts, business profiles, and CRM or booking system

  • Be available for the appointments booked, or ensure your team is
  • Keep your ad spend funded and running at the agreed level throughout the engagement
  • Give us accurate information about your services, pricing, and service area, and tell us promptly if any of it changes

Delays or gaps on any of the above may delay results and will be accounted for when assessing whether the guarantee period has been met.
