Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Vobet Media ("Vobet Media," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at vobetmedia.github.io (the "Site") and provides paid advertising, lead generation, and marketing services to service businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through the Site, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

By using the Site or submitting a form on it, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not submit your information to us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you submit directly. When you book a strategy call, fill out the onboarding form, or email us, we collect what you provide — typically your name, business name, email address, phone number, business address, website and social profile links, and details about your business, budget, and services.
  • Automatically collected technical data. Like most websites, the Site is served through infrastructure (currently GitHub Pages) that logs standard technical information for every visitor — such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and access times — for security and reliability purposes. We do not currently run analytics or advertising pixels on this Site itself.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and book strategy calls
  • To build, launch, and manage advertising campaigns on your behalf if you become a client
  • To send confirmations, scheduling details, and service-related communication
  • To improve this Site and the services we offer
  • To meet legal, accounting, or security obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the services described above.

3. How Form Submissions Are Handled

This Site does not operate its own server or database. Forms on the Site (including the booking form and the client onboarding form) are delivered using FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service that forwards submissions by email to Vobet Media. FormSubmit's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing it if you have questions about how a submission is transmitted before it reaches us.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site relies on a small number of third-party services to function:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts and serves this website.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on the Site. Requests for font files are made to Google's servers.
  • FormSubmit — delivers form submissions to us by email, as described in Section 3.

If you become a client, the advertising platforms we manage on your behalf — such as Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads — will process data under their own respective privacy policies and your own advertising account's settings. Those platforms are not covered by this policy.

5. Cookies

This Site does not currently set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Google Fonts may set minimal technical cookies as part of delivering font files. If we introduce analytics or advertising pixels on the Site in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

6. Data Retention

We retain information submitted through our forms for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, deliver our services, or comply with legal and accounting obligations. You can request that we delete information you have sent us at any time — see Section 8.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Submitting information through the Site, or through any third-party service it relies on, is done at your own risk.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children's Privacy

This Site and our services are directed at business owners and are not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at hello@vobetmedia.com.
