FAQ

The questions we get asked most.

How long before we see anything?

The build takes roughly two weeks — offer, creative, landing pages, tracking, and the follow-up system. Campaigns usually go live in week two or three, and first enquiries land within days of launch. Booked appointments follow once the follow-up sequences are running against real leads.

What does it cost?

A monthly management fee plus your ad budget, which stays in your own account. The right budget depends on your market, your job value, and how much work you can actually take on — so we set both together on the call, before you commit to anything.

Who writes the ads?

We do — every ad, funnel, landing page, and automation. You approve each ad before it goes live, so nothing runs under your name that you have not seen.

What do you need from us?

Access to your ad accounts and CRM, someone who can turn up to the appointments we book, and quick turnaround on ad approvals. Everything else sits with us.

Which trades do you work with?

Service businesses of every kind — roofing, landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, remodeling, windows, solar, concrete, fencing, painting and more. Vobet Media works best where a single job is worth enough to justify paid acquisition. If your numbers do not support it, we will say so on the call rather than take you on.

What if it does not work?

You get 120 qualified estimates within 90 days or we keep working free until you do. We would rather carry that risk than sell you a retainer that quietly underperforms.
