FAQ

The questions we get asked most.

How long before we see anything?

The build takes roughly two weeks: offer, creative, landing pages, tracking, email and SMS flows, and the follow-up system. Campaigns usually go live in week two or three, and the first data arrives within days of launch. We then improve it every week.

What does it cost?

A monthly management fee plus your ad budget, which stays in your own account. The right budget depends on your margins, your average order value or deal size, and how fast you want to grow, so we set both together on the call before you commit to anything.

Who writes the ads?

We do: every ad, landing page, email, SMS and automation. You approve each ad before it goes live, so nothing runs under your name that you have not seen.

What do you need from us?

Access to your ad accounts, store or CRM, and email/SMS platform, quick turnaround on approvals, and for B2B someone on your side who takes the meetings we book. Everything else sits with us.

Who do you work with?

D2C and ecommerce brands that want profitable new customers and more repeat orders, and B2B companies that want a steady pipeline of qualified meetings. If your numbers do not support paid growth yet, we will say so on the call rather than take you on.

Do you work with Shopify?

Yes. We work with Shopify and other store platforms, the main email and SMS tools, and the common B2B CRMs. If you already have a stack, we build on it rather than replace it.

Where is your growth leaking?

Book a call and we will review your ads, your funnel, your email and SMS, and your follow-up, and show you where customers and meetings are slipping away.
