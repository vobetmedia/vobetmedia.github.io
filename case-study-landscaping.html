LAWN & LANDSCAPING · TEXAS

52 Leads at $17.
15 Jobs Closed.

A residential lawn and landscaping owner ran founder-led and UGC creative alongside statics. 52 qualified leads at about $17 each across four ad sets — and because a custom form replaced Meta's instant forms, nearly every one booked an estimate.

THE CHALLENGE

Cheap leads that never became estimates.

  • Meta's instant forms produced volume, but the details were careless and a good share of the numbers went nowhere.
  • Polished, stock-style creative looked like every other lawn care ad in the feed.
  • Enquiries arrived with no context, so quoting meant starting the conversation from scratch.
  • Budget was tight — the campaign had to work on hundreds of dollars, not thousands.
WHAT WE DID

Traded polish for proof, and instant forms for intent.

  • Put the owner on camera — founder-led creative doing the selling.
  • Ran UGC-style clips against traditional statics to see which the market answered.
  • Replaced Meta's instant form with a custom form that asked for real detail.
  • Split four ad sets so creative type and audience could be judged separately.
WHY IT WORKED

Friction in the Form Was the Point

1 · A custom form, not an instant form

Meta's instant forms take one tap, so they collect people who were barely paying attention. A custom form asks for a little effort — and the people who finish it are the people who actually want an estimate.

2 · Founder-led creative

The owner on camera, on a real job, in his own words. Homeowners hiring someone to work on their property are buying the person as much as the service.

3 · UGC beat statics decisively

The two UGC ad sets produced 48 of the 52 leads at $13–$16 each. The statics and the base form managed 4 leads between them at $35–$56. The test settled the question in days, not months.

4 · Quality over volume

52 leads is a small number. Fifteen of them turned into paid work, which is the only number that pays wages. Chasing cheaper leads would have improved the first figure and ruined the second.
