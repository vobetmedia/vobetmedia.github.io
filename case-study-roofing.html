THE CHALLENGE

"I've tried roofing leads before. They were junk."

  • Enquiries went cold while the crew was up on a roof — the homeowner called the next company.
  • Estimates were quoted, then never chased. The job went to whoever followed up.
  • Bought leads were resold to three other roofers at the same time.
  • Demand swung between storm-season chaos and off-season silence.
WHAT WE DID

Built the acquisition system end to end.

  • Wrote and designed every ad, funnel, and landing page.
  • Set up the entire back end — tracking, CRM, booking, and automations.
  • Client approved every ad before it went live.
  • Switched on the Roof-to-Roof Follow-Up System across every lead and estimate.
THE SYSTEM

Four Parts, Running as One

1 · Automated Pipeline

Meta campaigns built for the service area, with weekly creative testing on live data. We write everything; the client approves every ad before a dollar is spent.

2 · Speed-to-Lead

Every enquiry gets a first response in minutes — qualified automatically and booked straight into the diary, seven days a week.

3 · The Roof-to-Roof Follow-Up System

The answer to "low quality leads". Every lead and every estimate is worked across email, SMS, and call tasks until it converts or explicitly says no. Nothing is written off after one attempt.

4 · Full Visibility

Cost per lead and cost per booked appointment tracked in the ad account itself, reviewed on a weekly call, with support inside 24 hours on business days.
