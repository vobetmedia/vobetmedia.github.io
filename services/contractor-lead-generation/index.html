Overview

What is contractor lead generation?

Contractor lead generation is the process of reliably attracting people who want to hire a contractor and turning their interest into booked estimates. It can come from referrals, directories, lead marketplaces, search ads, social ads or a mix.

Vobet Media's approach is to build a system you own: paid campaigns in your own ad accounts, a form that qualifies enquiries and automated follow-up that answers quickly. The target is booked, qualified estimates, which is the part advertising can control.

Honest view

Benefits and limitations.

What this service does well, and where you should set realistic expectations.

Benefits

  • Exclusive enquiries from campaigns you own, not shared marketplace leads
  • One system covering ads, qualification, follow-up and reporting
  • Costs you can track from lead to booked estimate to job
  • Assets — data, audiences, history — that stay with your business
  • Scalable up or down to match your capacity

Limitations

  • Results depend on your offer, market, pricing and how well estimates are run
  • A new system takes a few weeks to build and optimise
  • Lead volume is not the goal; booked, qualified estimates are
  • No outcome can be guaranteed beyond what the engagement terms state

Lead quality and tracking

How we think about lead quality and tracking.

We distinguish raw leads, qualified leads, booked estimates and jobs won, and report each separately. Quality is shaped by the offer, the qualifying form and speed of reply, and each stage is tracked so we can see where enquiries drop out and fix that stage first.

FAQ

Contractor Lead Generation questions, answered.

What is contractor lead generation?

Attracting and qualifying people who want to hire a contractor, then converting their interest into booked estimates, through channels such as search ads, social ads and follow-up systems.

How is this different from buying leads?

Marketplace leads are often shared and sold per enquiry. This approach builds campaigns you own, with exclusive enquiries and costs you can track to booked jobs.

How many leads will I get?

It depends on your market, budget and offer, so we do not promise a number upfront. We focus on qualified, booked estimates and set targets on the strategy call.

What is the guarantee?

Our standard guarantee is 120 qualified estimates within 90 days, or we keep working at no charge until you get them, under the terms in our Terms & Conditions.

Which industries do you work with?

Roofing, electrical, HVAC, plumbing, construction, remodeling, landscaping and solar, among other home service businesses.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You do. Campaigns run in your own ad accounts, so you keep the data and history.
