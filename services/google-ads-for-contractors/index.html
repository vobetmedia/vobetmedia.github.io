Overview

What is google ads for contractors?

Google Ads for contractors means paying to appear when someone searches for a service — 'roof repair near me', 'panel upgrade', 'emergency plumber'. The searcher already has a need, so intent is high and the window to win the job is short.

That makes Google the backbone for urgent and ready-to-book demand. It is often less suited to creating demand for planned projects that people have not started researching, where Meta Ads can reach them earlier.

Honest view

Benefits and limitations.

What this service does well, and where you should set realistic expectations.

Benefits

  • Captures high-intent homeowners at the moment they search
  • Strong for urgent, emergency and ready-to-book work
  • Easy to track calls and forms back to keywords
  • Budget can be limited to your service area and working hours
  • Compounds with other channels by catching demand they create

Limitations

  • Clicks in competitive trades can be expensive
  • Poor campaign structure wastes spend on low-value searches
  • Calls and forms still need fast, consistent follow-up to convert
  • Results vary by market, season and competition; costs are not guaranteed

Lead quality and tracking

How we think about lead quality and tracking.

Search ads attract varied intent, so lead quality comes from structure and filters: the keywords you target, the ones you exclude, how the landing page and form qualify visitors and how fast you respond. We report cost per lead and cost per booked appointment, and treat low-value call volume as a problem to fix, not a success.

FAQ

Google Ads questions, answered.

Are Google Ads worth it for contractors?

For many trades, yes, because they reach people already looking to hire. Whether they are profitable for you depends on your job values, close rate and how well calls and forms are followed up.

Do you have a Google Ads case study?

Not yet published. Our current case studies are Meta-based roofing and landscaping campaigns. We will publish a Google Ads case study only when we have verified results to show.

What is the difference between Google Ads and Local Services Ads?

They are separate Google products with different formats, eligibility and pricing. Our work focuses on Google search campaigns you own; some contractors also use Local Services Ads alongside them.

How do you avoid wasted clicks?

Through intent-based campaign structure, negative keywords, location and schedule controls, and landing pages and forms that qualify visitors.

How do you track phone calls?

Calls can be tracked with call-tracking numbers and call-conversion settings, alongside form submissions, so both are reported against campaigns.

Should I run Google Ads and Meta Ads together?

Often, yes. Google captures existing demand and Meta builds it earlier. We adjust the split as data shows which produces booked estimates for your trade.
