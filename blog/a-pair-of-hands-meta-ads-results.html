A Pair of Hands: 126 Leads in Four Months From One Meta Campaign

Four months of one campaign, read line by line from the ad account.

In short

A Pair of Hands’ New Leads campaign produced 126 Meta form leads at $29.24 each on $3,684.28 of spend between May 7 and Sep 6, 2026. It reached 23,933 people with 85,655 impressions, so each person saw the ads about 3.6 times on average.

Key facts
  • 126 Meta lead-form leads.
  • $29.24 cost per lead; $3,684.28 total spend.
  • May 7 to Sep 6, 2026.
  • 23,933 people reached; frequency about 3.6.

A Pair of Hands is a service business that ran one Meta lead campaign over four months. The case study shows the screenshot.

The numbers

  • 126 leads from Meta lead forms.
  • $29.24 cost per lead.
  • $3,684.28 total spend.
  • 85,655 impressions reaching 23,933 people, May 7 to Sep 6, 2026.

Reading the numbers

126 leads over about four months is an average of roughly 31 a month. Impressions divided by reach gives a frequency of about 3.6: the average person saw the ads between three and four times. The cost per 1,000 impressions was about $43.01.

Why frequency matters

Frequency is worth watching on long-running campaigns. A few views per person usually helps people remember and act; when it climbs much higher without new creative, results often slow down. Fresh creative keeps a long campaign working.

What this does not show

These are form leads, not booked jobs or revenue. The second campaign visible in the screenshot is a separate job-ad campaign with no spend in this period.
