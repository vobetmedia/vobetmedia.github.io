How the Vobet Media Follow-Up System Turns Enquiries Into Booked Jobs

Most lost jobs are not lost to a competitor's price. They are lost to silence. Here is how we close that gap.

In short

The Vobet Media follow-up system replies to new enquiries within minutes, books estimates where it can, and keeps chasing quotes that have gone quiet. It exists because the speed of the first reply and the discipline of the follow-up decide a large share of whether a lead becomes a booked job.

Key facts
  • New enquiries are answered within minutes.
  • The system books estimates and keeps chasing quiet quotes.
  • Speed of first reply and post-quote follow-up decide many jobs.
  • Landscaping case study: 52 leads, 15 jobs closed.

Ask a business owner why a lead did not turn into a job and you will hear about price, timing or "tyre-kickers." Look closer and the real cause is often simpler: nobody replied quickly, and nobody followed up after the quote went out. That is the gap our follow-up system is built to close.

The first reply

People enquiring about a service are usually contacting several businesses at once. The one that answers first, clearly and politely, tends to set the terms of the conversation. Our system replies to new enquiries within minutes — including evenings and weekends, when you are on a job or off the clock — and moves the person towards booking an estimate.

The booking

Because the qualifying form has already collected what you need to know, the first reply can be specific rather than generic. It confirms the details, offers a time and gets the estimate on your calendar without a string of back-and-forth messages.

The follow-up after the quote

This is the part most businesses do inconsistently, simply because they are busy. Quotes go out, the customer goes quiet, and the job quietly dies. The system keeps checking in at sensible intervals so a quote is never left to go cold just because nobody had time to chase it.

Why it matters for ad spend

Every lead costs money to generate. A slow or missing reply wastes that money before the conversation has even started. Fast, consistent follow-up is one of the cheapest ways to improve the return on the ads you are already paying for — which is why we treat it as part of the campaign, not an optional extra. Our landscaping case study shows the result in numbers: 52 leads, 15 jobs closed.

Common questions

How fast does the system reply to new enquiries?

Within minutes of the enquiry arriving, day or night.

Does it replace my sales conversation?

No. It gets the estimate booked and keeps quotes warm; you still do the estimate and close the job.

What happens when a quote goes quiet?

The system follows up at sensible intervals so the customer is reminded without you having to chase manually.
