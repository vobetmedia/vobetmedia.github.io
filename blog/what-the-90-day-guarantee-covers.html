What the 90-Day Guarantee Actually Covers (and What It Doesn't)

A guarantee is only worth something if you know exactly what it promises. Here is ours, with the fine print said out loud.

In short

The Vobet Media guarantee is 120 qualified estimates within 90 days, or we keep working at no charge until you get them. It covers booked, qualified estimates — the part we control — not whether a customer signs the contract afterwards. The full conditions are set out in the terms.

Key facts
  • Guarantee: 120 qualified estimates in 90 days, or Vobet Media keeps working free until delivered.
  • Counts: booked, qualified estimates from the qualifying form.
  • Does not promise signed contracts.
  • Full conditions are in the Terms & Conditions.

Plenty of agencies advertise a guarantee, and many of them are impossible to claim on. We would rather explain ours plainly, including the limits, so you can decide whether it is worth anything to you.

What it promises

Our standard guarantee is 120 qualified estimates within 90 days, or we keep working free until you get them. If we fall short, you do not get a refund-shaped apology — you get more work from us, at no charge, until the number is hit.

What counts as a qualified estimate

A qualified estimate is a real, local enquiry that has been through the qualifying form and has booked an estimate or inspection with your business. It is not a raw form fill, a click or a name on a list. That is deliberate: it ties the guarantee to the outcome you actually care about, not to a vanity number that is easy to inflate.

What it does not cover

The guarantee does not promise that every estimate becomes a signed job. How you price, how you present the quote and how you follow up in person are in your hands, not ours. We can control the supply of qualified, booked estimates; we cannot sign the contract on your behalf. Scoping the guarantee to what we control is what lets us offer it without hedging.

Why we offer it at all

We only take clients where we believe the numbers can work, and we say so on the first call if they cannot. A guarantee backed by that kind of selectivity is a risk we are comfortable carrying. The exact conditions are set out in our terms, and we are happy to walk through them before you sign anything.

A guarantee you can't understand is a guarantee you can't use.
