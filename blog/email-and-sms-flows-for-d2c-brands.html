Email and SMS Flows Every D2C Brand Should Have

Paid ads bring people to your store once. Email and SMS bring them back, without paying for the attention again.

In short

Every D2C brand should run five core email and SMS flows: welcome, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase and win-back. They run automatically, reach people you have already paid to acquire, and turn one-time buyers into repeat customers, which makes paid acquisition far more profitable.

Key facts
  • Five core flows: welcome, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase, win-back.
  • Flows run automatically once built.
  • They reach customers you have already paid to acquire.
  • Weekly campaigns sit on top of the flows.

Most D2C brands spend most of their marketing budget getting someone to the store for the first time, and very little on what happens next. Email and SMS flows fix that. Once built, they run in the background and keep bringing people back.

1. Welcome flow

New subscribers are at their most interested right after they sign up. A short welcome series introduces the brand, answers common questions and gives them a reason to make the first purchase.

2. Browse abandonment

Someone looked at a product and left. A timely reminder of what they viewed, with reviews or answers to likely objections, brings a share of them back.

3. Cart abandonment

People who added to cart and did not check out are the warmest audience you have. A short sequence by email and SMS recovers orders that would otherwise be lost.

4. Post-purchase

After the first order: thank them, help them get the most out of the product, ask for a review, and suggest what to buy next. This is where one-time buyers start becoming repeat customers.

5. Win-back

Customers who have not ordered in a while get a reason to come back, before they forget you entirely.

How flows work with paid ads

Paid ads bring people in; flows turn them into customers and repeat customers. Together they make the cost of acquiring a customer far easier to pay back. See how we approach the paid side in Meta ads for D2C brands.

Common questions

Which email flows should a D2C brand set up first?

Welcome, cart abandonment and post-purchase, then browse abandonment and win-back.

Should we use SMS as well as email?

Usually yes, for time-sensitive messages like cart abandonment, with clear consent.

Do flows replace weekly campaigns?

No. Flows run automatically; weekly campaigns sit on top for launches and offers.
