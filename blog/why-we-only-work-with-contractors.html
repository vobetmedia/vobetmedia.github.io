Why Vobet Media Only Works With Service Businesses

Most agencies will take on anyone with a card that doesn't decline. We turned that model down on purpose — here's why narrowing to one type of client made us better at serving it.

Early on, it's tempting to say yes to every enquiry. A skincare brand wants email flows, a SaaS company wants outbound, a roofer wants leads — and a growth agency that says yes to all three looks like it's growing fast. What it's actually doing is building three shallow playbooks instead of one deep one.

We made a deliberate call to stop doing that. Vobet Media works with service businesses — roofing, landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, remodeling, and the rest of the trades — and nothing else. Here's the reasoning.

Every industry has a different failure point

An e-commerce brand's growth problem is usually creative fatigue or a leaky email flow. A B2B SaaS company's problem is usually sales cycle length. A service business's problem is almost never the ad platform — it's speed and follow-up. The enquiry comes in while the crew is on a roof, nobody answers for six hours, and the homeowner has already called the next name on the list.

That's a completely different problem to solve than the ones e-commerce or SaaS clients have. An agency serving all three either builds one generic system that half-fits everyone, or three specialist systems and pretends it's one team. We'd rather build the one system that actually fits, and only offer that.

Specialization compounds

Every roofing account we run teaches us something that makes the next roofing account better — which creative angle a homeowner responds to, what a fair cost per lead looks like in a given metro, how long a follow-up sequence needs to run before a quote goes cold. None of that transfers from a skincare account. It does transfer from one landscaping account to the next.

Our roofing case study and landscaping case study exist because we can point at real numbers from real accounts in the exact industry a prospect is calling us from — not an analogous industry, not a "similar" client. That's only possible because we didn't spread across ten verticals.

It lets us make a real guarantee

You can only promise 120 qualified estimates in 90 days, or we work free until you get them, when you actually know what "qualified" looks like for the trade you're running the campaign in, and how long a system like this takes to spin up. That confidence comes from repetition inside one category — not from running eight different playbooks for eight different industries and hoping one of them fits your next client.

The fastest way to become mediocre at ten things is to try to be excellent at all of them at once.

Who this isn't for

If you're not a service business, we're genuinely not the right fit — and we'd rather tell you that on the first call than take your budget and hand you a generic playbook with your logo on it. If you are, that focus is exactly what you're paying for.
