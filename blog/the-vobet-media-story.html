The Vobet Media Story

Why a founder who started in digital marketing at 13 built an agency around one idea: ads, follow-up and sales should work as one system.

In short

Vobet Media is a growth partner for D2C and B2B brands, founded by Kartik Sharma. It joins up paid ads, email and SMS, B2B lead generation and AI automation into one system, runs everything in accounts the client owns, and shows results as unedited ad-account screenshots.

Key facts
  • Founded by Kartik Sharma, who started in digital marketing at 13.
  • Works with D2C / ecommerce brands and B2B companies.
  • Ads run in the client’s own accounts.
  • Results are published as unedited ad-account screenshots.

Vobet Media started with a simple observation. Brands were paying for traffic that worked, and then losing the value of it somewhere between the click and the sale. The ad did its job. The follow-up, the email, the landing page or the sales handover did not.

Where it started

Kartik Sharma began learning digital marketing at 13, one campaign at a time: how ads, funnels and follow-up actually behave when real money is on the line. Over four years the same pattern kept showing up. The brands that grew were not the ones with the cleverest ad. They were the ones where every part of the system was connected.

What Vobet Media does now

Vobet Media works with two kinds of client. D2C and ecommerce brands that want profitable new customers from Meta and Google, and more repeat orders from email and SMS. And B2B companies that want a steady pipeline of qualified meetings from lead generation and outbound. Underneath both sits AI automation that qualifies and follows up with every lead in minutes.

How we work

  • Your accounts. Ads, pixels, email lists and CRM data live in accounts you own. If we ever part ways, everything stays with you.
  • You approve the creative. Nothing runs under your name that you have not seen.
  • One connected system. Ads, landing pages, flows, follow-up and reporting are built together, so one team is responsible for the whole path from click to customer.

Why we publish the real numbers

Most agency websites show stock photography and vague percentages. Ours shows actual Meta Ads Manager screenshots from client accounts, because we would rather be judged on what an account actually did than on a claim nobody can check.
