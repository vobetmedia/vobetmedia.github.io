The Vobet Media Story

Why we built a growth agency for one type of client, and why the guarantee that came out of it is a bet we're comfortable making.

Vobet Media started from a pattern we kept seeing on the client side of the table before we ever ran a campaign of our own: a service business owner pays for "leads," the leads arrive, and almost none of them turn into booked jobs. Not because the traffic source was bad — but because nobody was answering the phone fast enough, and nobody was chasing the estimate after it went out.

That gap is what most lead-gen products don't touch. They sell you a form fill and call it done. We built Vobet Media to own the part that actually decides whether a lead becomes revenue: the speed of the first reply, and the discipline of the follow-up after it.

What we actually build

Every engagement starts the same way — an audit of your offer, your service area, and what's currently converting or not. From there we build the ad campaigns, a qualifying form that filters out people who were never going to book, and an automated follow-up system that answers new enquiries in minutes and keeps chasing quotes that would otherwise go cold. You can see the exact shape of that system on our How It Works page.

None of that is exotic. It's the same discipline a well-run in-house sales team would apply — we just make it run automatically, at the speed a homeowner comparing three quotes actually expects.

Why we publish the real numbers

Most agency websites show stock photography and vague percentages. Ours shows the actual Meta Ads Manager screenshots from client accounts — cost per lead, appointments booked, spend, all of it — because we'd rather be judged on what the account actually did than on a claim nobody can check. When we say a campaign produced 33 booked estimates in two months, that number is a screenshot, not a projection.

Why the guarantee exists

Our standard guarantee is 120 qualified estimates in 90 days, or we keep working free until you get them, under the terms outlined here. We can offer that because we only work inside a category we deeply understand, and because the guarantee is scoped to what we actually control — booked, qualified estimates, not whether a homeowner signs the contract afterward. We'd rather carry real risk on a promise we understand than sell a vague retainer and hope it works out.

If the numbers on your account don't support a paid acquisition budget, we'll say so on the first call. We'd rather lose the deal than take a client we can't actually help.

That's the whole model: one category, one system, numbers we're willing to publish, and a guarantee scoped to what we can actually be accountable for.

Want to see if your numbers support this kind of system? It's the first thing we check.
