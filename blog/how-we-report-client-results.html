How We Report Client Results (and Why We Use Screenshots)

A screenshot can be checked. A percentage on a sales page cannot.

In short

Vobet Media reports client results with unedited ad-account screenshots, the exact date range and currency, and a written breakdown of every number. We report leads as leads and do not present them as booked jobs or revenue, and we do not publish results we cannot show from the account itself.

Key facts
  • Results are shown as unedited ad-account screenshots.
  • Every case study states the date range and currency.
  • Leads are reported as leads, not jobs or revenue.
  • Numbers are written out so they can be checked.

Marketing results are easy to exaggerate. "300% growth" and "10x ROAS" fit nicely on a sales page and are impossible to check. We do it differently.

1. Unedited screenshots

Each case study shows a screenshot straight from the client’s Meta Ads Manager, so you can see the campaign, the columns and the totals as the platform reports them.

2. The exact date range and currency

Every result states its period (for example Sep 10 to Oct 22, 2026) and currency, so nothing is stretched or blended.

3. Every number written out

Below each screenshot we write out the figures in a table and explain them: cost per lead, total spend, impressions, reach, and simple calculations like blended cost per lead or frequency.

4. What we do not claim

A lead is a form submission. We do not present leads as booked appointments, jobs or revenue unless we can show those numbers too. That keeps every claim checkable.
