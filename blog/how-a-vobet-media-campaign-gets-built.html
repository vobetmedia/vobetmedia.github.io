How a Vobet Media Growth System Gets Built, Step by Step

From the first audit to the first orders and meetings: what we build, in what order, and why each piece is there.

In short

A Vobet Media build starts with an audit of the offer, funnel and data, then sets up creative, landing pages, tracking, email and SMS flows (for D2C) or lead forms and outbound (for B2B), plus AI follow-up. Campaigns launch once every piece is connected, and the system is improved every week after that.

Key facts
  • Starts with a funnel and data audit.
  • D2C builds include email and SMS flows; B2B builds include lead forms and outbound.
  • AI follow-up is connected before launch.
  • Optimised weekly after launch.

Most underperforming accounts were not built badly piece by piece. They were built as separate pieces: an ad agency here, an email freelancer there, a CRM nobody connected to anything. We build the whole system in one order, so each piece feeds the next.

1. Audit and growth plan

We look at your offer, margins, average order value or deal size, past campaigns and the data you already have. The output is a plan: which channels, what budget, and what we will build first.

2. Creative, landing pages and tracking

We write and design the ads and the pages they land on, then set up tracking so every purchase, lead and booked meeting is measured in your own accounts. Without clean tracking, optimisation is guesswork.

3. The follow-up layer

For D2C brands this is email and SMS: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back flows. For B2B companies it is qualifying lead forms, outbound sequences and AI follow-up that replies to every lead in minutes and books meetings into your team’s calendar.

4. Launch

Campaigns go live across Meta, Google and, for B2B, LinkedIn. Because the follow-up layer is already running, the first visitors and leads are handled properly from day one.

5. Weekly optimisation

Every week we test creative, audiences, offers and flows. What works gets more budget; what does not gets cut. You see the same numbers we do, straight from your accounts.

Common questions

How long does the build take?

Roughly two weeks, with campaigns usually going live in week two or three.

Do you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We build on your store, email and SMS platform and CRM where they already work.

What happens after launch?

Weekly testing and optimisation across creative, audiences, offers and flows.
