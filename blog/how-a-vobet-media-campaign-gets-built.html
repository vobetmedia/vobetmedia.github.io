How a Vobet Media Campaign Gets Built, Step by Step

From the first audit to the first booked estimate: what we build, in what order, and why each piece is there.

In short

A Vobet Media campaign has four parts: an audit of your offer and service area, Meta and Google ads that run in your own accounts, a custom qualifying form in place of instant forms, and an automated follow-up system that replies in minutes. Each part exists to turn a click into a booked, qualified estimate.

Key facts
  • Four parts: audit, Meta and Google ads, custom qualifying form, automated follow-up.
  • Campaigns run inside the client's own ad accounts.
  • Reporting covers spend, leads and booked jobs weekly.
  • Goal metric: booked, qualified estimates.

When a new business signs with Vobet Media, the first question is usually "what actually happens now?" The honest answer is that the work is less mysterious than most agencies make it sound. There are four parts, they are built in a set order, and each one exists for a reason you can check against your own numbers.

1. The audit

Before any ad goes live we look at your offer, your service area and what is already converting or not. A good offer in the wrong area, or a vague offer in the right one, will quietly waste ad spend no matter how well the campaign is built. The audit is where we decide what to promote, who to promote it to, and what a single job needs to be worth for paid acquisition to make sense.

2. The ads

We build and run Meta and Google campaigns inside your ad accounts, so you own the data and the history from day one. The creative is built around the real work you do — founder-led and user-style video tends to beat polished stock imagery for local businesses — and each ad set is tied to one clear offer rather than a generic "get a quote" message.

3. The qualifying form

Instant forms make it far too easy for anyone curious to submit a lead. Instead, we send people to a custom form that asks the questions you would ask on the phone: what they need, where they are, and when they want it done. Fewer people get through, but the ones who do are far more likely to be real, local and ready to book. It is the single biggest reason our cost-per-lead and our lead quality can both look healthy at once.

4. The follow-up system

A lead that waits an hour for a reply is already talking to your competitor. The follow-up system replies to new enquiries within minutes, books the estimate where it can, and keeps chasing quotes that have gone quiet. This is the part most lead-gen vendors skip, and it is where a large share of lost jobs are actually decided.

What you see along the way

You get weekly reporting of spend, leads and booked jobs in one place, drawn from the same ad accounts you own. Our roofing and landscaping case studies show exactly what that reporting looks like, with the real Meta Ads Manager screenshots behind the numbers.

Every piece of the system is there to answer one question: did this turn into a booked, qualified estimate?

Common questions

How long does it take to build a campaign?

The audit and build come first, and ads go live once the offer, creative, qualifying form and follow-up system are ready. We confirm the exact timeline on your strategy call because it depends on how quickly creative and account access are in place.

Whose ad accounts do the campaigns run in?

Yours. Campaigns run in your own Meta and Google accounts, so you keep the data, the audiences and the history whether or not you keep working with us.

Do I have to answer every lead myself?

No. The follow-up system replies to new enquiries in minutes and books estimates where it can, so you spend your time on the work instead of chasing leads.
