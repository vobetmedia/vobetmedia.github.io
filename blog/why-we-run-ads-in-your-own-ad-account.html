Why We Run Your Ads in Your Own Ad Account

Most agencies run ads from their own accounts and hand you a report. We think that gets the ownership backwards.

In short

Vobet Media runs every campaign inside the client's own Meta and Google ad accounts. The business owns the data, audiences and history, can see spend in real time, and keeps everything if the engagement ends. Ad budget stays in the client's account; the agency charges a separate management fee.

Key facts
  • Vobet Media runs every campaign in the client's own Meta and Google accounts.
  • Ad budget stays in the client's account; management fee is separate.
  • The client keeps data, audiences and history if the engagement ends.
  • Results can be checked against the source accounts at any time.

There is a quiet but important difference between agencies. Some run your ads from their own ad accounts and send you a monthly PDF. Others build everything inside your accounts. We do the second, and it is not negotiable — here is why.

You own what the money builds

Every pound or dollar spent on ads builds something: audience data, pixel history, learnings about which creative and offers work. If that all lives in an agency's account, it walks out the door when they do. In your account, it stays with the business that paid for it.

You can see everything, any time

Because the campaigns live in your Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads, you can log in and check spend, leads and results whenever you like. Our weekly reporting pulls from those same accounts, so the numbers you get from us can be checked against the source. It is also why our case studies use real ad-account screenshots rather than summary figures.

Your ad budget stays yours

Under our model the ad budget stays in your own account, and we charge a separate management fee. That separation keeps things honest: there is no markup hidden inside the media spend, and you can see exactly what is going to Meta and Google.

Leaving is simple

If we are not the right fit, or the engagement simply comes to an end, you keep the accounts, the audiences and the history. Nothing needs to be exported or handed over, because it was never ours. We would rather earn the next month than trap you in it.

If an agency won't run your ads in your own account, ask them why.

Common questions

Who owns the ad account if I stop working with Vobet Media?

You do. The accounts, audiences and campaign history belong to your business from the start, so nothing has to be handed back or transferred.

Does the ad budget go through Vobet Media?

No. The ad budget stays in your own Meta and Google accounts and we charge a separate management fee.

Can I see the results myself?

Yes. You can log in to your own ad accounts at any time, and our weekly reporting draws from the same data.
