Why We Run Your Ads in Your Own Ad Account

Most agencies run ads from their own accounts and hand you a report. We think that gets the ownership backwards.

In short

Vobet Media runs every campaign in the client’s own Meta, Google and LinkedIn ad accounts, connected to the client’s own store, email platform and CRM. The client owns the data, the pixel history and the creative, can check results at any time, and keeps everything if the engagement ends.

Key facts
  • Campaigns run in the client’s own ad accounts.
  • The client owns the pixel data, audiences and creative.
  • Results can be checked in the platform at any time.
  • Nothing is lost if the engagement ends.

Some agencies run campaigns from accounts they own and send you a monthly report. It looks convenient until you want to leave, and discover that the pixel history, audiences and learnings that took months to build belong to someone else.

What we do instead

Every campaign we run lives in your own Meta, Google and, for B2B, LinkedIn ad accounts. Tracking connects to your own store, email and SMS platform and CRM. We work inside your accounts with access you grant and can remove.

Why it matters for D2C brands

For an ecommerce brand, the pixel and purchase history are some of its most valuable assets. They are what lets the platforms find more buyers like your best customers. That history should compound for you, not for an agency.

Why it matters for B2B companies

For a B2B company, the leads, audiences and conversion data feed your pipeline and your CRM. Keeping them in your own accounts means your sales team, your reporting and any future partner can use them.

It keeps us honest

Because the campaigns live in your accounts, you can log in and check spend, orders, leads and results whenever you like. It is also why our case studies use real ad-account screenshots rather than summary figures.

Common questions

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside it with access you grant.

Can I see the campaigns myself?

Yes, at any time, directly in the ad platform.

What happens if we stop working together?

You keep everything: accounts, data, audiences and creative.
