Harper Tree Services: 55 Leads at £3.68 on a £5 Daily Budget

A small budget, one campaign and six weeks. Here is what it produced, straight from the ad account.

In short

Harper Tree Services ran one Meta lead campaign on a £5 daily budget between Sep 10 and Oct 22, 2026. It produced 55 form leads at £3.68 each, £202.32 in total, from 25,342 impressions. That is roughly 27 leads for every £100 of ad spend.

Key facts
  • 55 Meta lead-form leads.
  • £3.68 cost per lead; £202.32 total spend.
  • £5 daily budget, Sep 10 to Oct 22, 2026.
  • 25,342 impressions; about £7.98 per 1,000 impressions.

Harper Tree Services is a tree surgery business. Its campaign is a useful example of what a small, focused Meta budget can do. The case study has the unedited screenshot.

The numbers

  • 55 leads from Meta lead forms.
  • £3.68 cost per lead.
  • £202.32 total spend on a £5 daily budget.
  • 25,342 impressions, Sep 10 to Oct 22, 2026, with 7-day click attribution.

What the numbers mean

£202.32 bought 55 leads, roughly 27 leads for every £100 of ad spend. The cost per 1,000 impressions worked out at about £7.98 (£202.32 ÷ 25,342 × 1,000).

Why a small budget can work

A low daily budget is not a handicap when the offer is clear and the audience is local and specific. It also makes testing cheap: the whole six-week run cost about the same as a single small job.

What this does not show

These are form leads reported by Meta, not booked jobs or revenue. How many become customers depends on how quickly and how well each lead is followed up.
