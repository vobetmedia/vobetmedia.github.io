Western Roof Tiling: 133 Leads From Meta Ads, Explained

Two campaigns, one account, very different costs per lead. Here is what the numbers in the screenshot actually say.

In short

Western Roof Tiling’s two Meta lead campaigns produced 133 form leads on $3,350.79 of ad spend, a blended $25.19 per lead. The New Leads campaign brought in 102 leads at $21.44 each; Campaign 2, still running on a $25 daily budget, brought in 31 leads at $37.54 each. All figures are lifetime totals from an unedited Meta Ads Manager screenshot.

Key facts
  • 133 Meta lead-form leads in total.
  • $3,350.79 total ad spend; blended $25.19 per lead.
  • New Leads campaign: 102 leads at $21.44.
  • Campaign 2: 31 leads at $37.54, $25/day budget.

Western Roof Tiling is a roof tiling business that runs Meta lead campaigns. The full case study shows the unedited Ads Manager screenshot. This article explains what those numbers mean.

The headline numbers

  • 133 leads from Meta lead forms across two campaigns.
  • $3,350.79 total ad spend.
  • $25.19 blended cost per lead ($3,350.79 ÷ 133).

The date range in the screenshot is set to "Maximum" (Aug 23, 2023 to Sep 23, 2026), so these are lifetime totals for each campaign.

Why two campaigns landed so differently

The New Leads campaign produced 102 leads at $21.44 each on $2,186.98 of spend, reaching 21,890 people with 64,285 impressions. Campaign 2, which is still live on a $25 daily budget, produced 31 leads at $37.54 each on $1,163.81 of spend, reaching 6,021 people with 20,244 impressions.

Same business, same service, same platform, and a cost per lead that differs by about 75%. That gap is normal. Audience, creative and offer decide which people a campaign reaches, and that is why every account needs ongoing testing rather than a set-and-forget build.

What this does and does not show

The screenshot shows leads: form submissions reported by Meta. It does not show booked appointments, jobs won or revenue, and we do not claim those here. Lead cost is the first number in the chain; what happens after the form (speed of reply, follow-up, sales process) decides how many become paying customers.
