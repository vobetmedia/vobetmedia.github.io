Why Vobet Media Works With D2C and B2B Brands

Different buyers, same problem: turning attention into customers, efficiently and repeatably.

In short

Vobet Media works with D2C ecommerce brands and B2B companies because both need the same thing: paid acquisition that pays back, follow-up that never lets a buyer go cold, and data they own. The channels differ (email and SMS for D2C, lead generation and outbound for B2B), but the system and the discipline are the same.

Key facts
  • D2C focus: profitable new customers and repeat orders.
  • B2B focus: a steady pipeline of qualified meetings.
  • Both use paid ads, follow-up, AI automation and owned data.
  • One connected system, adapted to each model.

We are often asked why an agency would work with both D2C brands and B2B companies. The short answer: the buyers are different, but the problem is the same.

What D2C brands need

D2C and ecommerce brands need new customers at a cost that pays back, and a reason for those customers to buy again. That means Meta and Google campaigns optimised for purchases, and email and SMS flows that build repeat revenue.

What B2B companies need

B2B companies need a steady pipeline of qualified meetings, not a spreadsheet of names. That means lead generation on LinkedIn, Meta and Google, outbound that reaches the right accounts, and fast follow-up that books meetings for the sales team.

What both have in common

  • Paid acquisition that is judged on revenue and pipeline, not clicks.
  • Follow-up that reaches every lead or shopper in minutes, not days.
  • AI automation that does the repetitive work so the team does not have to.
  • Data that lives in accounts the brand owns.

One system, adapted

Under the hood, the system is the same: audit, build, launch, follow up, optimise every week. What changes is the channel mix and what we optimise for. Read how a growth system gets built.

Common questions

Does Vobet Media work with ecommerce brands?

Yes. D2C and ecommerce brands are one of our two focus areas.

Does Vobet Media work with B2B companies?

Yes. We run lead generation, outbound and AI follow-up for B2B companies.

Is the approach different for D2C and B2B?

The system is the same; the channel mix and what we optimise for are different.
