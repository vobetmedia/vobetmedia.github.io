FAQ

Plumbing marketing questions, answered.

Is Google Ads or Facebook better for plumbers?

Google Ads usually capture urgent, high-intent demand, while Meta Ads suit planned work such as water heater upgrades and repipes. Many plumbers use both, weighted to their capacity and job mix.

How do you stop paying for low-value calls?

By structuring campaigns around the jobs you want, using negative keywords, setting service areas and schedules, and qualifying enquiries with a short form or call script before a technician is dispatched.

Can you generate more water heater and repipe jobs?

Yes, those are good fits for targeted campaigns. They tend to be higher-value and often planned, so qualifying questions and a booked estimate matter more than raw call volume.

What about after-hours enquiries?

Automated replies can acknowledge new enquiries within minutes at any time, gather details and offer the next available slot, so out-of-hours demand is not simply lost.

Do you replace Local Services Ads or directory listings?

No. Those are separate channels and some plumbers use them alongside their own ads. Our focus is campaigns you own, plus the qualification and follow-up around them.

How is plumbing lead quality measured?

By how many enquiries become booked jobs and what they are worth, not just by cost per lead. Weekly reporting ties spend and leads to bookings.
