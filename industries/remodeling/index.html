The challenge

What holds back growth in remodeling businesses.

Built for remodelers, kitchen and bath specialists and renovation firms who want serious homeowners, not price-shoppers, booking design consultations.

Price-shoppers and tyre-kickers

Big projects attract casual browsers. A qualifying form helps surface budget range, scope and timing early.

Long decision windows

Homeowners often spend months planning a remodel. Without nurture, a good lead can be forgotten before they commit.

Inconsistent project pipeline

Strong months followed by gaps make it hard to schedule crews and trades. Inbound campaigns add predictability.

Slow replies on high-value enquiries

With big-ticket projects, the first company to respond professionally often earns the consultation.

Unclear return on ad spend

It can take weeks or months for an ad click to become a signed project. Tracking needs to follow the whole journey.

FAQ

Remodeling marketing questions, answered.

Are Meta Ads good for remodeling companies?

Often yes, because remodeling is visual and homeowners plan well ahead. Strong project imagery and a clear offer can reach them before they have chosen a contractor, with Google Ads covering those already searching.

How do you filter out price-shoppers?

Through a qualifying form that asks about the project, rough budget range and timing, and through follow-up that sets expectations before an in-home consultation is booked.

How do you handle a long decision cycle?

With automated and personal follow-up over weeks or months — reminders, helpful content and check-ins — so the lead remains warm until they are ready to commit.

Do you design the creative?

We create the ads and landing pages, and use your real project work wherever possible. You approve each ad before it goes live.

What can I expect in the first months?

Campaign build and launch, first enquiries within days of going live, and then iterative improvement. Remodeling projects take longer to close, so booked consultations are the first measure.

How do you report on remodeling results?

Weekly reporting covers spend, leads and booked consultations, and we track signed projects as they close so you can see the full return over time.
