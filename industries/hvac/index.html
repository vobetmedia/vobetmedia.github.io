The challenge

What holds back growth in hvac businesses.

Built for independent HVAC companies and small teams who want replacement and installation work, not just emergency calls, and who need leads qualified before a technician drives out.

Seasonal peaks and quiet months

Summer and winter bring floods of calls; shoulder seasons can feel empty. Campaigns need to flex with demand and your capacity.

Emergency calls versus high-value installs

A no-cooling call and a full system replacement are different customers. Messaging and forms should separate them so the right jobs get priority.

Speed-to-lead in an emergency

A homeowner with no heating calls several companies at once. The first useful reply usually wins the job.

Competition from large brands and marketplaces

National brands and lead marketplaces spend heavily on search. Local trust, clear offers and quick response help smaller companies compete.

Unclear return by job type

Repairs, replacements and maintenance plans have very different values. Tracking by job type shows where advertising really pays.

FAQ

HVAC marketing questions, answered.

What is the best way to market an HVAC company?

A combination: Google Ads for urgent, search-driven demand, Meta Ads for planned replacements and off-season work, and fast follow-up so every enquiry gets a reply. Our guide on HVAC marketing strategies covers seasonal planning in more detail.

How do HVAC ads handle seasonality?

Budgets, offers and messaging shift with the season — cooling in summer, heating in winter, maintenance and heat pumps in between — and are capped to what your team can actually service.

Can ads separate repair calls from replacement leads?

To a degree. Campaign structure, ad messaging and a qualifying form can distinguish a quick repair from a system replacement, so you can prioritise and route enquiries accordingly.

How does AI follow-up help in an HVAC emergency?

It replies within minutes, collects the key details and offers appointment or dispatch slots, which matters when a homeowner is contacting several companies. Your team still handles the job itself.

Do you only run emergency campaigns?

No. Emergency searches are one part. We also build campaigns around installations, replacements, heat pumps and maintenance agreements, which can support steadier revenue.

How is performance measured for HVAC?

By booked estimates and booked jobs per campaign, not just leads. Weekly reporting shows spend, leads and bookings, ideally split by job type.
