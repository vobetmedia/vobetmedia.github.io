The challenge

What holds back growth in landscaping businesses.

Built for landscaping companies, lawn care businesses and outdoor specialists who want installs and recurring plans, not a stream of one-off mowing price checks.

Strong seasonality

Spring and summer are packed; winter is quiet. Campaigns should build the pipeline ahead of the season and match your capacity.

Low-ticket enquiries crowding out good jobs

Price-driven mowing enquiries can bury larger design and installation projects. Qualification helps you focus on the work you want.

Tight service areas

Route density drives profit. Targeting should keep enquiries within the area your crews can serve efficiently.

Slow replies in peak season

When crews are busy, enquiries sit unanswered. Automated first replies keep homeowners engaged until you can respond.

Limited tracking of profit by job type

Maintenance plans, installs and hardscaping have different margins. Tracking by job type shows where advertising pays.

FAQ

Landscaping marketing questions, answered.

Do Facebook ads work for landscaping companies?

They can. In our published landscaping case study, Meta ads with a custom form produced 52 leads and 15 closed jobs on $877 of ad spend for one Texas client. That is one result in one market, not a promise.

How do you plan around seasonality?

By starting campaigns ahead of peak season, adjusting budget and messaging through the year, and capping spend to what your crews can handle.

Can you help me get installs rather than just mowing?

Yes. Campaigns can be built around design, installation and hardscaping offers, and a qualifying form helps separate project enquiries from one-off maintenance price checks.

What is the benefit of a custom form over Meta's instant forms?

A custom form adds a little friction, which reduces casual submissions and collects better information. In the landscaping case study, that trade-off produced fewer but better leads.

How do you measure landscaping results?

From ad to form to booked estimate to closed job, with weekly reporting. Maintenance plans and installs are tracked separately where possible.

How do you keep leads inside my service area?

Through geographic targeting, location questions on the form and follow-up that confirms the address before booking.
