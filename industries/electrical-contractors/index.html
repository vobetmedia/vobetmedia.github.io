The challenge

What holds back growth in electrical businesses.

Built for residential electrical contractors and small electrical crews in the US and UK who want a steadier flow of planned, higher-value jobs instead of relying on referrals and repeat customers alone.

Inconsistent inbound leads

Some weeks the phone rings constantly; others it goes quiet. Without a repeatable source of enquiries, scheduling crews and planning cash flow becomes guesswork.

Too much reliance on referrals

Referrals are great but you cannot turn them up when the calendar is thin. Paid acquisition gives you a lever you control.

Competition for high-value installs

Panel upgrades, EV chargers and whole-home rewires attract several competing quotes. The business that replies first and looks credible usually gets the estimate.

Missed calls and slow follow-up

Electricians are on site, in an attic or behind a panel — not at a desk. Enquiries that wait hours for a reply are often booked with someone else.

No clear view of marketing ROI

Many contractors cannot say which ads produced booked estimates versus tyre-kickers, so they cannot tell what to spend more on.

FAQ

Electrical marketing questions, answered.

How much should an electrical contractor spend on advertising?

There is no single right number. Work backwards from the jobs you want: your average job value, the share of estimates you close, and how many extra jobs your crew can take on. That sets a realistic cost-per-job target and, from it, a monthly budget. We cover this on the strategy call rather than quoting a generic figure.

Are Facebook Ads effective for electricians?

They can be, particularly for planned work such as EV chargers, panel upgrades and lighting where homeowners are not yet searching. Results depend on the offer, the creative and how quickly enquiries are answered. Google Ads usually complement them by capturing people already searching.

What types of electrical jobs can digital ads generate?

Both planned and urgent residential work: panel upgrades, EV charger installs, rewiring, repairs, generators and lighting. Higher-value jobs generally suit a qualifying form and a booked estimate rather than a quick phone call.

How does AI lead follow-up work?

When an enquiry arrives, the system replies within minutes, asks the qualifying questions you care about and offers appointment times. It is there to book the estimate, not to replace you — you still attend and quote the job. See our guide on AI appointment setting for the detail.

How long does it take to launch campaigns?

The build — offer, creative, forms, tracking and follow-up — typically takes around two weeks, and campaigns usually go live in week two or three. First enquiries tend to arrive within days of launch, though timing varies by market.

How do you measure booked appointments?

Enquiries are tracked from ad click to form to booked estimate, and you receive weekly reporting on spend, leads and booked jobs. We separate booked estimates from jobs won, because the first is what advertising controls and the second also depends on your quote.
