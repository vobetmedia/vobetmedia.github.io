FAQ

Roofing marketing questions, answered.

How much do roofing leads cost?

It varies by market, offer and lead source, so any single figure is a guess. Shared marketplace leads are priced per enquiry; running your own ads gives a cost per lead you can track. What matters more is cost per booked inspection and per job. Our guide to roofing lead costs walks through the maths.

Are shared roofing leads worth buying?

Often not at the advertised price. If several roofers receive the same enquiry, your chance of winning depends on who dials first and on price. Owning your own campaigns builds assets and gives exclusive enquiries.

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for roofers?

Yes, they can. In our published roofing case study, Meta ads plus a fast follow-up system produced 74 leads and 33 booked inspections over two months for one US business. That is one client's result, not a guarantee or a benchmark.

What about storm-damage work?

Storm demand is time-sensitive, so campaigns should be ready to launch quickly and replies must be fast. We plan the creative, forms and follow-up in advance so you can respond when weather hits, subject to local rules on storm marketing.

How do you reduce inspection no-shows?

By confirming appointments, sending reminders and making it easy to reschedule. Automated SMS and email reminders do the repetitive work so your team is not chasing every homeowner by hand.

How is booked-inspection performance measured?

Each enquiry is tracked from ad to form to booked inspection, with weekly reporting on spend, leads and appointments. Jobs won are tracked separately because they also depend on your estimate.
